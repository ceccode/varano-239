VARANO 2:39

Privacy notice

Last updated: 27 August 2026

VARANO 2:39 is a free game that runs entirely in your browser. There are no accounts, forms, subscriptions, adverts, profiling cookies, or cross-site tracking.

What stays on your device

Game progress, preferences (language, character, audio, text size and contrast), your personal best, the collection of played levels and the endings you have already reached are stored in your browser's local storage. They are not sent to a server and are not visible to us. You can remove them at any time with “Delete local progress and preferences” in the game menu, or by clearing the site's data in your browser.

The shareable score card is also generated on your device and is never uploaded to our servers.

Aggregate statistics

We use GoatCounter, an open-source, cookie-free analytics service, to understand in aggregate how visits become games, completions and replays. Only these fixed signals are sent:

  • a visit, with a fixed page address and title;
  • a game start, with no additional properties;
  • arcade completion of levels 1, 3, 6 and 10;
  • reaching an ending, without saying which one;
  • clicking share or replay, without the content or result.

We do not send your chosen character, decisions, ending, score, language, settings, screen size or a persistent identifier. Skipping a level does not send its completion milestone. For the referring site, we retain at most its domain (for example https://example.com), never the complete address.

If your browser sends a Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control signal, analytics are disabled entirely and the script is not loaded. The game works identically when analytics are blocked.

Hosting and automated decisions

There is no profiling, automated decision-making or sale of data. The site is hosted by Netlify, which processes the technical data required to deliver its pages, such as IP addresses in server logs, as the hosting provider.
